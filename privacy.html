Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026. Your privacy and data integrity are fundamental to Westpoint Security. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to operate the portal securely and verify authorized personnel:

  • OAuth2 Account Identity (Roblox & Discord): User ID, username, avatar reference, and group/server role assignments. We do not receive or store your passwords.
  • Citizen Submissions: Form data submitted voluntarily via the Reach Out to Staff form or Misconduct Reporting intake (e.g. contact name, handle, topic, incident descriptions).
  • Operational Logs: Duty shift timers, incident reports logged by officers, and audit timestamps.
  • Technical Logs: IP address and request metadata retained temporarily for sliding-window anti-DDoS protection and security monitoring.

2. How We Use Information

Collected data is used strictly for:

  • Verifying security guard ranks, supervisor clearances, and executive command access.
  • Synchronizing duty presence with our real-time operational roster and Discord logs.
  • Investigating citizen misconduct reports through Internal Affairs.
  • Defending portal infrastructure against unauthorized access, payload tampering, and denial-of-service attempts.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We interact with trusted third-party APIs solely to support portal functionality:

  • Roblox API / OAuth2: Used to confirm user identities and group rank status.
  • Discord API / OAuth2: Used for officer identification and sending departmental event logs via webhooks/bot integration.

4. Data Storage & Security Controls

We implement defense-in-depth protections including HTTPS encryption, Content Security Policies (CSP), 256-bit cryptographically random session identifiers, sliding-window rate limiting, and atomic data storage.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of stored inquiry logs or session data at any time by contacting our administration at privacy@westpointsecurity.xyz or submitting a formal request via the Contact Desk.
