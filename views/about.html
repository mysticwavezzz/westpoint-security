About Westpoint Security

Westpoint Security is a licensed private security contractor providing protective services throughout Harrison County.

Harrison Branch Organization

Established in 1994, the Harrison County Regional Branch operates from our Rockdale Command Station, managing event security details, private protective assignments, and commercial static guarding.

Command & Supervision

All operations are overseen by active Field Supervisors, Sergeants, and Command Staff adhering to municipal oversight policies.

Code of Professional Conduct

Officers are held to strict standards regarding professional impartiality, disciplined de-escalation, and proportional force management. Any violations are subject to internal review.
